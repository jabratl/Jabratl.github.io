Privacy// the short version

Some of my apps offer Sign in with Google. It is always optional or used only for the one job you asked for. Thazir uses it for backup to its own private folder in your Drive, and Bulk Replace uses it to read and edit the Google Docs you choose.

I do not sell, share or use your Google data for ads or to train AI. Full policies: Thazir · Bulk Replace. You can remove any app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
